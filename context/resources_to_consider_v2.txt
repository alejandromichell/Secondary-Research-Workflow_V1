Resources To Consider

Company & Industry Intelligence

Company Information:


* SEC EDGAR Database (sec.gov/edgar) - Public company filings, 10-Ks, 10-Qs, proxy statements
* Google Finance - Company financials, stock performance, key metrics
* Yahoo Finance - Financial data, analyst ratings, company profiles
* Crunchbase (free tier) - Startup and private company data, funding rounds
* OpenCorporates - Global corporate registry data
* Companies House (UK) - British company filings and accounts


Industry Research:


* Statista (free tier) - Statistics, market data, industry insights
* Pew Research Center - Consumer trends, demographic data, social research
* Trade association websites - Industry-specific reports and white papers
* Industry journals and publications - Often offer free articles and insights


Market & Competitive Analysis


* Google Trends - Search trends, geographic interest, related queries
* Builtwith - Technology stack analysis for competitors
* Alexa Internet Archive (Wayback Machine) - Historical website data
* Product Hunt - New product launches and innovations
* G2/Capterra - Software reviews and competitive comparisons


Economic & Market Data


* Federal Reserve Economic Data (FRED) - Economic indicators, financial data
* Bureau of Economic Analysis - GDP, economic growth, industry data
* Bureau of Labor Statistics - Employment, wages, productivity data
* World Bank Open Data - Global development and economic indicators
* IMF Data - International financial statistics
* OECD Data - Economic statistics for developed countries
* Census Bureau - Demographic and economic census data


News & Current Events


* Google News - Current events, company news, industry updates
* Reddit (industry subreddits) - Community insights, sentiment analysis
* LinkedIn - Company updates, executive movements, thought leadership
* Twitter/X Advanced Search - Real-time sentiment, trending topics
* Press release distribution sites (PR Newswire, Business Wire) - Company announcements


Academic & Research Resources


* Google Scholar - Academic papers, research studies, citations
* SSRN (Social Science Research Network) - Working papers, research
* ResearchGate - Academic publications and researcher networks
* PubMed - Life sciences and biomedical research
* arXiv - Pre-print scientific papers


Patent & Innovation Analysis


* Google Patents - Patent search and analysis
* USPTO Patent Database - US patent filings
* WIPO Global Brand Database - Trademark information
* European Patent Office (Espacenet) - European patent data


Financial Analysis Tools


* Finviz - Stock screener, financial visualizations
* Macrotrends - Long-term financial trends and ratios
* MarketWatch - Market data and financial news


Consumer & Social Insights


* Trustpilot - Customer reviews and satisfaction data
* Glassdoor - Employee reviews, company culture insights
* Indeed Company Reviews - Workplace insights
* App Store/Google Play reviews - Product feedback analysis


Government & Regulatory Resources


* USA.gov - Federal government data and reports
* Data.gov - US government open data
* FTC Consumer Sentinel - Consumer complaint data
* FDA databases - Drug approvals, medical device data
* EPA databases - Environmental compliance and violations


International Resources


* Eurostat - European statistical data
* Trading Economics - Global economic indicators
* UN Data - International development statistics
* Country-specific government databases - Local market data


AI-Powered Research Tools


* Perplexity AI - AI-powered search and summarization


Specialized Tools


* Internet Archive - Historical web pages and documents
* Think tanks (Brookings, RAND, etc.) - Policy research and analysis
* University research centers - Industry-specific studies
* Kaggle Datasets - Data for analysis projects
* GitHub - Open-source projects, technology trends


Pro Tips for Effective Secondary Research:


* Cross-reference multiple sources for validation
* Check publication dates to ensure current relevance
* Use library resources - Many public/university libraries offer free access to premium databases
* Set up Google Alerts for ongoing monitoring
* Archive important findings as web content can disappear
* Document your sources systematically for citations
* Look for original sources rather than aggregated summaries
* Use advanced search operators in Google (site:, filetype:, etc.)
